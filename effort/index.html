            What changed

            Join this effort

            Joining isn't open yet. It opens once Threadlands can run it safely: a private record in place, a tested check of who can see what, consent recorded, and a simple way to leave.

            When it opens, joining shows publicly only as a count by neighborhood, and never for fewer than three people. There are no profiles, follower counts or feed. People who join get a short note when the effort moves a step.
