ThreadLab · Compare climate responses

Learn, analyze and plan with real geographic data.

The data behind every place profile, in depth, for planners, practitioners, researchers, students, city officials, and anyone who wants to learn GIS. Pick a place to start, or choose a tool.

    1 Explore Analyze a whole city region. See heat, flooding, farmland and nature together, test the city against a heatwave, a flood, a drought and 2050, and let Threadlands find the plan that does the most for your goal. Take a costed decision brief with you. Open Explore → 2 Studio Design it on real streets. Draw parks, urban farms, ponds, tree lines and sea walls on the real map, in 3D, with local costs, upkeep and carbon. Run a workshop where teams take turns on one screen. Open Studio → 3 Method How it works, and how far to trust it. The open data behind every map, how the model scores a place, and its limits, written so you can check it or explain it to a council.     A typical path

    1. Start from a city. Its profile shows what presses on it and what it depends on.
    2. Explore the region. Pick a goal, such as cooler streets or drier homes, and compare plans.
    3. Design in Studio. Turn the plan into real shapes on real streets, with costs.
    4. Take it to the people who decide. Download the brief, the map for GIS, or share a link.
