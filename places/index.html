Your priorities

The overall index is one provisional way of reading the data. Move a slider and the index and the order change, because different priorities lead to different judgments.

Water and low-carbon travel are also in the index, but every place starts at almost the same score: the model measures them against each place’s own today, so they move only when you test a plan in ThreadLab. To compare water between places now, use Flood-prone homes. Equity and affordability are not measured yet.

About the comparisons

What goes in

Heat relief, flood exposure, connected nature, local food and low-carbon travel, plus three stress tests (a heatwave, a river flood and a drought year). Each is modeled for the whole city region from open satellite, land cover and population data.

Real projects

Parks, wetlands and other projects with a public source are placed on the map. Those approved, under way or built since 2021 count toward the score. Older ones already show in the 2021 land cover.

Read it as a first look

This is a screening index, not a verdict. Flood and travel are not yet fully comparable between places, and some places are outlined larger than the city itself. We are working on both, and the index will change as the data improves.

Read cell-based comparisons with care. Heat hot spots, green cover, tree cover and flood-prone homes are counted in grid cells, and cell sizes differ between places, from about 0.1 km² to over 25 km². Results change with cell size, so each row shows its grid, and you can compare only places with a similar grid. Read the known limitations →
