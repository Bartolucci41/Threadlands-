Rankings

How prepared are cities for heat, floods and drought?

The Threadlands resilience index for every place in the library, from 0 to 100. Real projects that are approved, under way or recently built count toward each city’s score.

Sort by

    How the index works

    What goes in

    Heat relief, flood exposure, connected nature, local food and low-carbon travel, plus three stress tests (a heatwave, a river flood and a drought year). Each is modeled for the whole city region from open satellite, land cover and population data.

    Real projects

    Parks, wetlands and other projects with a public source are placed on the map. Those approved, under way or built since 2021 count toward the score. Older ones already show in the 2021 land cover.

    Read it as a first look

    This is a screening index, not a verdict. Flood and travel are not yet fully comparable between places, and some places are outlined larger than the city itself. We are working on both, and the index will change as the data improves.
