Get involved

Follow your places. Help shape what happens next.

Follow the places you live in, come from, or care about to keep up with them. When you want to be counted, stand with a place: every person who does shows city teams and funders that its people are paying attention.

  1. Followa place to keep up with its warnings, news and projects
  2. Stand witha place to be counted as one of its supporters
  3. Backthe projects you want to see happen
  4. Addwhat you know: a project or a sourced update
