Get involved

Stand with your places. Help shape what happens next.

Stand with the places you live in, come from, or care about: the city you’d fight for first, and any others close to your heart. Every person who stands with a place shows city teams and funders that its people are paying attention.

  1. Stand witha place, and get its warnings and news
  2. Backthe projects you want to see happen
  3. Addwhat you know: a project, a problem, an event
  4. Leadlook after a place and check what others add (coming soon)
