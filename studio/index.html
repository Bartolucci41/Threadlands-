Studio

Redesign a real place, and see what it does by 2050.

  1. Pick a place.
  2. Choose an element (an urban farm, trees, a pond) and click the map. Zoom in close to draw exact shapes on real streets.
  3. Press Play to watch it grow to 2050, and try the challenges.

Workshop: design together

For a room of people around one screen. Each team takes turns placing elements; the scoreboard shows what each team spent and what it added.

Region view

You’re in 3D. Buildings stand at their real heights, from OpenStreetMap, or estimated where they aren’t mapped.

  • Look around: drag to move, scroll or + and − to zoom.
  • Build: pick something from the menu above, then click a building, lot or street.
  • Watch it grow: press Play; trees grow and new buildings rise toward 2050.
