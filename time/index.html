How this is made, and the sources

The old lakes are traced from elevation: every grid cell at or below 2,250 m that connects to the center of the old island city, the high-water line used by López-López and colleagues (2023). In our cells that comes to about km². Published estimates of the lakes’ size range from about 1,000 to 2,000 km². Today’s ground has sunk in places, so the outline is a reconstruction, not a survey. The shrinking lake between 1607 and 1980 is not mapped year by year.

Today uses the same Threadlands data as the Mexico City page: ESA WorldCover 2021, WorldPop 2020, Landsat surface heat from 2021 to 2025, and cells of about km².

The future is one scenario run in the Threadlands model, on the middle climate path for 2050: wetlands on unbuilt land of the old lakebed (about km², close to the size of the Parque Ecológico Lago de Texcoco) and 20% more tree cover on streets. It is a test of an idea, not a forecast. Try your own in ThreadLab →
