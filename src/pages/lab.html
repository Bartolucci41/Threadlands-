    A typical path

    1. Start from a city. Its profile shows what presses on it and what it depends on.
    2. Explore the region. Pick a goal, such as cooler streets or drier homes, and compare plans.
    3. Design in Studio. Turn the plan into real shapes on real streets, with costs.
    4. Take it to the people who decide. Download the brief, the map for GIS, or share a link.
