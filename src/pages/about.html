About Threadlands

Why it exists

Cities are usually planned inside their own borders. But the water a city drinks falls on hills far upstream, its food grows on farms it never sees, and the forests and wetlands that cool it and soak up its floods often sit outside its limits.

Threadlands puts the city and that land on one map, so a planner, a council or a neighborhood group can see the ties, test a plan against heat, flooding, drought and the climate of 2050, and know where to act first. It runs on open global data, so it works for any place on Earth, and it is free to use.

Who made it

Threadlands is created by Robert Bartolucci. Robert has worked in corporate sustainability for about fifteen years and is now focused on the future of cities: urban sustainability, planning and infrastructure, and the healthy connection cities need with rural areas and the ecosystems around them. Threadlands is where those ideas meet.
