The library

Every place that opens instantly in Threadlands. Each one has measured satellite heat, the river basin it drinks from and the farmland around it. Don’t see yours? Search for it on the Explore page and build it in about ten minutes.

Zoom with + and −, your scroll wheel or a double-click, and drag to move. Names and smaller places appear as you zoom in. Dot size shows population. Map outlines from Natural Earth, public domain.
